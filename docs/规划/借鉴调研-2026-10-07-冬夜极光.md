原文（逐字摘）：
> The aurora, also known as the northern lights, is a natural phenomenon that occurs in the night sky in the
> Animal Crossing series beginning in Animal Crossing: City Folk. In Animal Crossing: City Folk and
> Animal Crossing: New Leaf, the aurora is visible on **clear winter nights**, and can be seen from **8 PM
> until 10 PM**. It is at its **brightest at 9 PM**, shining bright shades of **pink and green** and
> **moving constantly in a folding motion**. In Animal Crossing: New Horizons, it can appear in any hour
> with clear conditions between 7 PM and 4 AM, as long as there are no shooting stars that night.
> Residents of the player's town may comment on the aurora when it is in the sky.

## 三 · 口径（本单怎么用）

1. **抄哪一版**：取「City Folk／New Leaf」那档——**晴冬夜 20:00–22:00、21:00 最亮**（时段与峰值都清楚、可被探针量）；
   New Horizons 的「7PM–4AM 随时出现、当夜有流星就不出现」**不取**——本作第 164 单的流星是**每晚都有**的常设项，
   照 NH 那条会把极光几乎全挡掉。

3. **村民会不会提**：出处说「居民可能评论极光」——本作**本单不做**（要动 SIM 台词层），登记为候选。
